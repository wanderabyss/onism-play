# Code of Conduct

This repository is a place for bug reports, feature ideas and release notes for Onism Play. Keep it friendly and useful.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Stay on topic and give enough detail for someone to help.
- Don't post other people's private information, account details, passwords or logs that contain them.

## What isn't welcome

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Spam, advertising or unrelated links.
- Requests for pirated games or ways around a store's rules.

## Enforcement

Comments or issues that break these rules may be edited, closed or removed, and repeat offenders may be blocked. To report a problem, email play@onism.ai.
